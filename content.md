## About
I'm an AI researcher currently pursuing an Advanced Master's in Artificial Intelligence at KU Leuven, specializing in the Speech and Language track. My research focuses on NLP for low-resource and classical languages, especially Sanskrit. This includes fine-tuning LLMs, building datasets, and improving low-resource machine translation and cross-lingual retrieval. Before KU Leuven, I worked at IIT Kharagpur in the Complex Networks Research Group (CNeRG) and the Visual Information Processing Lab, and in industry on applied machine learning.
